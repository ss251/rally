## Scan protocol (mental tools — not findings)

[Feynman: RotatingVault] One contract holds many savings circles. People take turns getting the pot. Nobody gets paid unless everyone paid that turn. If a turn ends short, the circle dies and everyone still owed money can pull it back. No owner.

[Feynman: createCircle] Organizer opens a circle: which token, how much each person puts in each turn, how long a turn lasts, how many seats. Nothing is deposited yet.

[Feynman: redeemInvite] Anyone can deliver the organizer's signed ticket. The ticket names the member and which turn they get paid. That person now occupies that seat. A used ticket number cannot be used again. A ticket with no expiry, or an expiry already passed, is refused.

[Feynman: start] Organizer starts the clock once every seat is filled. Turns are then just "how much time has passed."

[Feynman: cancel] Organizer can shut the circle only before it starts — no money has ever been held.

[Feynman: deposit / depositFor / _deposit] A member (or someone paying for them) puts in exactly one payment for the turn that is open right now. The vault checks it actually received that exact amount. Paying for someone else can only help them.

[Feynman: claim / claimFor / _claimTo] The person whose turn it is pulls the full pot, but only after every seat paid that turn. Anyone can press the button; the money still goes to that person. If this was the last unpaid pot, the circle is finished.

[Feynman: markBroken / _ensureBroken] Anyone can write down that a circle already died (a finished turn was short). Refunds do this automatically too.

[Feynman: refund / refundFor / _refund] After death, a member pulls back everything they put in minus their share of pots that already left. Anyone can press the button; money still goes to that member.

[Feynman: _elapsedRounds] How many full turn-lengths have passed since start.

[Feynman: _lazyBroken] The circle is dead if the most recently finished turn is short. Deposits only go into the live turn and stop the moment this is true, so an older short turn implies this one is short too.

[Feynman: _firstFailedRound] Walk seats from the start and name the first short turn (for the event).

[Feynman: EIP-712 helpers / eip712Domain / inviteDigest / domainSeparator] Build the invite fingerprint wallets sign. If the chain id changes, rebuild it so old-chain tickets do not work here.

[Feynman: RotatingVault views] Read stored circle, members, current turn, windows, pot size, payee, funded flag, refundable amount. `circleStatus` treats a stored-active but short-last-turn circle as dead. `getCircle` does not.

[Feynman: GoalVault] Crowdfund escrow. Same-chain backers push tokens in. Cross-chain, Circle drops tokens on the vault and a trusted relayer writes down who they belong to. Hit the target: beneficiary pulls the pot. Miss: backers pull their money back, locally or burned back to the chain they came from. Vault token balance must always cover the running "we owe this much" number.

[Feynman: constructor / setRelayer / setTokenMessenger / setCctpRefundParams] Owner names the token, this chain's Circle domain, who may attribute mints, optional burn router, and burn fee knobs.

[Feynman: rescueExcess] Owner may sweep tokens that arrived but were never written down as anyone's — only the slack above "we owe this much," and only after every campaign's late-arrival window has closed.

[Feynman: createCampaign] Anyone opens a fund: target, when it closes, who is paid on success. Pushes the global rescue lock out to that close plus one hour.

[Feynman: contribute] Same-chain backer sends tokens. Vault credits whatever actually arrived. Closes at the deadline or once the pot has been withdrawn.

[Feynman: recordContribution] Relayer writes down a mint that already landed. Allowed until deadline plus one hour, unless the pot was already withdrawn. Refuses if the vault does not already hold that extra token.

[Feynman: withdraw] Beneficiary or creator pulls the campaign's full credited total once the target is hit — even before the deadline. That also slams the door on later credits.

[Feynman: refund / refundFor / _refund] After a miss, a backer (or the relayer for them) gets their credited total on this chain. Credited total is zeroed first.

[Feynman: refundCrossChain] After a miss, the backer or the relayer burns each remote-chain slice back to the same address on that chain, and sends the local slice here. Irreversible once the burn lands.

[Feynman: _credit] Add to the campaign total, the global owe-number, the backer's totals by chain, and the log. Fire "goal hit" the first time the target is crossed.

[Feynman: _requireOpen] Still accepting same-chain money: not withdrawn and before the deadline.

[Feynman: _requireCreditable] Relayer may still write down a mint: not withdrawn and before deadline plus one hour.

[Feynman: _requireFailed] Refunds only if not withdrawn, deadline reached, and credited total still under the target.

[Feynman: GoalVault views] Campaign fields, derived status (target-hit wins over "deadline passed"), refundable flag, per-backer totals, contribution log.

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:530 — why?] Why does "failed" mean `raised >= goal` is impossible, when `raised` is never reduced by a refund? Implicit belief: after the deadline, `raised` is frozen. That belief is false — `recordContribution` can still add to `raised` for another hour.

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:375 — why?] Why does withdraw send `c.raised` rather than "tokens still sitting here for this campaign"? Implicit belief: nothing can leave a campaign except this withdraw. Refunds break that.

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:522 — why?] Why is "already withdrawn" enough to refuse a late mint write-down? Implicit belief: a closed campaign has no remaining claimant for that mint. The burner on the other chain still exists.

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:310 — why?] Why does one campaign's deadline push a single global rescue clock? Implicit belief: every campaign's late mint window is independent except for this max. A far-future campaign then shelters every other campaign's stray tokens from rescue.

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:190 — why?] Why does `DeadlineTooFar` exist if `createCampaign` never uses it? Implicit belief: a cap was planned (the frontend even clamps to 60 days) and the on-chain side was left open.

[Socratic: RotatingVault.sol:338 — why?] Why measure the incoming balance change but not the outgoing claim/refund? Implicit belief: a token that delivered exactly `A` inbound will also send exactly `A` outbound.

[Socratic: RotatingVault.sol:490 — why?] Why is "most recently finished turn is short" enough to declare the whole circle dead? Implicit belief: no one can ever have funded a later turn after an earlier short turn. Holds only because `_deposit` consults this check first.

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:114 — why?] Why can the owner set `cctpMaxFee` to any size? Implicit belief: the backer chose the cross-chain refund path. False — the relayer can force it.

[Inversion: recordContribution] (1) After deadline, credit just enough to cross the goal after Alice already refunded — `raised` jumps, refunds die, withdraw underflows. (2) After an early withdraw, credit a mint that landed 10 minutes later — `CampaignClosed`, mint becomes slack. (3) Credit slack from campaign A's mint onto campaign B — documented relayer mis-attribution.

[Inversion: withdraw] (1) Hit goal with a same-chain payment while Base CCTP burns are in flight, withdraw immediately — those mints can never be written down on this campaign. (2) Withdraw after variant (1) of `recordContribution` — `totalEscrowed -= raised` underflows. (3) Withdraw to a recipient that refuses the token — revert; backers also cannot refund because `raised >= goal` (self-chosen beneficiary).

[Inversion: _requireFailed / refund] (1) Refund at the exact deadline on a miss — allowed. (2) Refund after a late credit crossed the goal — `NotFailed`, money stuck if anyone already refunded. (3) Refund, then let the relayer credit the same backer under the goal — second refund works; the dangerous case is only the cross.

[Inversion: createCampaign] (1) `deadline = type(uint64).max` — `rescueUnlockTime` becomes ~year 584e9, `rescueExcess` is dead. (2) Open a 60-day campaign after others have settled — re-locks rescue of their stray mints. (3) `deadline = now+1` — legal; grace is only one extra hour.

[Inversion: _lazyBroken] (1) Warp past several empty turns — last finished turn is short, circle dies, unclaimed earlier pots dissolve (documented). (2) Try to pay the next turn after a short turn — deposit sees dead and reverts. (3) Claim an earlier full pot after a later short turn — claim sees dead and reverts.

[Inversion: _deposit] (1) Fee-on-transfer inbound — delta ≠ `A`, revert. (2) Pay twice in one turn — `AlreadyDeposited`. (3) Pay after the last turn's window — `CircleExpired` or `CircleIsBroken`.

[Inversion: _claimTo] (1) Claim before everyone paid — `RoundNotFunded`. (2) Stranger `claimFor` the payee — money still goes to the payee. (3) Claim years later on a healthy circle — allowed (pots do not expire).

[Inversion: refundCrossChain] (1) Relayer forces it for an email-wallet backer — irreversible burn to that address on the origin domain. (2) Owner sets `cctpMaxFee` near `amount` — destination mint is haircut. (3) Messenger unset on a remote slice — whole call reverts, local slice not paid either (atomic).

[Inversion: rescueExcess] (1) Call before the global unlock — `RescueLocked`. (2) Call when slack is 0 — `ZeroAmount`. (3) Call when a deadlock left tokens inside `totalEscrowed` — slack math treats them as owed; owner cannot unstick them.

[Inversion: contribute] (1) Pay after deadline — `CampaignClosed` even inside the relayer's grace hour. (2) Pay after goal but before withdraw — allowed; overshoot goes to the beneficiary. (3) Pay 0 after a fee-on-transfer token ate everything — `ZeroAmount`.

## RotatingVault — seams that closed

Deposit → claim → break → refund is internally consistent: one deposit per member per live turn; claim requires `fundedCount == N`; break checks the last finished turn; refund floor is `claimedCount * A`. Unclaimed pots on a later default dissolve into refunds (documented). Permissionless `claimFor` / `refundFor` / `depositFor` cannot redirect value. Ownerless — no rescue analogue, no shared clock to grief. The `MAX_ROUND_DURATION` cap is the pattern GoalVault's deadline is missing.
