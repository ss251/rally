## Scan protocol (mental tools — not findings)

[Feynman: RotatingVault] One contract holds many savings circles. An organizer opens a circle, people take reserved payout turns via signed invites, the clock starts when every seat is filled, and each open window everyone puts in the same amount. The person whose turn it is gets the whole pot only after every seat paid that turn. If a finished window is short, the circle dies and each person pulls back whatever they still have here, minus the share that already left in pots that were fully paid out.

[Feynman: GoalVault] One contract holds many crowdfunds. Same-chain backers send USDC in themselves. Cross-chain, Circle drops USDC on the vault and a trusted relayer writes down who it belongs to. Hit the target: the beneficiary takes the credited pile. Miss: each backer gets their credit back here or burned back to the chain they came from. The advertised safety net is “vault token balance always covers the running owe-number.”

[Feynman: createCircle] Organizer names the token, the exact per-person amount, how long a turn lasts, and how many seats. The contract hands out the next id and stores those rules. The pot (amount × seats) is checked so it can never overflow later. No money moves.

[Feynman: redeemInvite] Anyone can deliver a signed ticket. The ticket names the member, their payout turn, a one-time number, and an expiry. The contract fills that seat and burns the number. Rights sit on the named member, not the caller.

[Socratic: RotatingVault.sol:251 — why?] Why reject only a zero expiry and not a far-future one? Implicit belief: the organizer’s signature is consent to that lifetime. A leaked ticket with expiry = max lives until cancel (only while filling, no funds held).

[Feynman: start] Organizer, only while filling and full, flips the circle to running and stamps “now” as the start. Turns are then just “how much time has passed.”

[Feynman: cancel] Organizer shuts a circle only before it starts. No money has ever been held. After start this door does not exist.

[Inversion: cancel] (1) Organizer takes an early pot then tries to cancel to unwind later seats — no abort after start. (2) Organizer cancels a full unpaid circle — seats die, no funds at risk. (3) Stranger calls cancel — not the organizer.

[Feynman: _deposit / deposit / depositFor] Only a running, not-dead circle. Credit goes to `member`. Tokens are pulled from the caller. The live turn is how many full windows have passed. One exact payment per person per turn. A short-received transfer is rejected. Paying for someone else can only increase their credit.

[Socratic: RotatingVault.sol:317 — why?] Why pull from the caller but credit the member? Implicit belief: the payer is gifting the seat. Refunds later pay the member, not the payer.

[Inversion: _deposit] (1) Pay a future turn in advance — live turn is derived from the clock only. (2) Pay after a finished turn was short — the death check trips first. (3) Pay a non-member — rejected.

[Feynman: _claimTo / claim / claimFor] The payee’s seat number *is* the turn. That turn must be fully funded and not yet paid. Mark paid, bump the paid-turn counter, send amount × N to the payee. Last pot flips the circle to finished. Anyone may press the button; money still goes to that payee.

[Inversion: _claimTo] (1) Pass someone else’s address — you pay *them*, never yourself. (2) Claim a funded pot after a later turn failed — death check blocks; the pot becomes refunds. (3) Claim twice — already-paid flag blocks.

[Feynman: _refund / refund / refundFor] Circle must be dead (stored or derived). Pay the member “lifetime deposits minus paid-turns × amount.” Write their deposited total down to that floor. Anyone may trigger it; tokens only go to them.

[Inversion: _refund] (1) Refund while the last finished turn is full — not dead. (2) Refund twice — held is already at the floor. (3) Refund a member who never deposited — nothing to pay.

[Feynman: _ensureBroken / markBroken] If the circle is already marked dead, no-op. If it is running and the death check is true, write Broken and emit the first short turn. Otherwise refuse.

[Feynman: _elapsedRounds] How many full turn-lengths have passed since start. Uncapped — can exceed the seat count.

[Feynman: _lazyBroken] A circle is dead iff the most recently *finished* turn is short. Deposits only hit the live turn and stop the moment this trips, so a later finished turn being short means an earlier failure already happened (or this one is the first). The first window never counts as finished.

[Socratic: RotatingVault.sol:490 — why?] Why only the last finished turn, not a full scan? Implicit belief: nobody can fund a later turn after an earlier one failed. That holds only if every deposit path calls this check — both `deposit` and `depositFor` do.

[Inversion: _lazyBroken] (1) Fund turn 0, skip ahead several windows — later turns sit at 0, last-finished is short, circle dies, unclaimed turn-0 pot becomes refunds. (2) Try to fund turn 2 after turn 1 died — deposit sees dead and reverts. (3) All turns full, clock past the end — last-finished is full, not dead; deposits expire, claims still work.

[Feynman: _firstFailedRound] Walk seats from the start and name the first short turn (for the event). Reverts if none are short.

[Feynman: RotatingVault views] `getCircle` returns stored fields (status may still say running after a short finished turn). `circleStatus` and `refundableAmount` apply the death check. `currentRound` is uncapped elapsed time. `roundWindow` rejects a turn ≥ N. `payeeOf` returns the zero address for a turn ≥ N. `isRoundFunded` is “this turn’s counter equals N,” even after death. `potOf` is amount × seats.

[Feynman: GoalVault.createCampaign] Anyone opens a fund: target, a future close time, who is paid on success. Pushes the global rescue lock out to that close plus one hour.

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:190 — why?] Why does `DeadlineTooFar` exist if create never uses it? Implicit belief at write time: a far close would pin the rescue lock forever. The check was forgotten.

[Feynman: contribute] Same-chain backer, campaign still open (not swept, now before the close). Pull tokens, credit whatever actually arrived, tag it as this chain.

[Feynman: recordContribution] Relayer only. Campaign not swept and still inside close+1h. Vault balance must already cover existing owe-number plus this amount. Credit the named backer and chain. No token pull.

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:516 — why?] Why is the relayer window an hour looser than same-chain pay-in? Implicit belief: a burn that left before the close may mint late, and that money should still count. Implicit belief that is *not* encoded: nobody has already been refunded, and the campaign total still equals money still held for this campaign.

[Feynman: _credit] Add to the campaign total, the global owe-number, the backer’s totals by chain, and the log. Fire “goal hit” the first time the target is crossed.

[Feynman: withdraw] Creator or beneficiary. Not yet swept. Campaign total ≥ target. Pay the *entire historical* campaign total to the beneficiary and drop the global owe-number by that same number.

[Inversion: withdraw] (1) Sweep a miss — total still under target, refused. (2) Sweep twice — already-swept flag. (3) After a miss-refund, a late credit pushes the historical total over the target — sweep pays the stale historical total, which is larger than this campaign’s remaining pile.

[Feynman: GoalVault._refund / refund / refundFor] Miss only (close passed, total still under target, not swept). Zero the backer’s credit and every per-chain bucket. Drop the global owe-number by that credit. Send the full credit to the backer on this chain. **Do not touch the campaign total.**

[Feynman: refundCrossChain] Backer or relayer. Same miss check. Zero the backer’s credit up front. Pay each chain locally or burn it back to the backer on that chain. **Do not touch the campaign total.**

[Inversion: _requireFailed] (1) Refund before the close — blocked. (2) Refund after the target is hit — blocked. (3) Refund after a miss, then a grace credit hits the target — further refunds now see “total ≥ target” and die, even though money is still sitting here.

[Feynman: _requireOpen / _requireCreditable / _requireFailed] Three different doors. Same-chain in: before close, not swept. Relayer in: before close+1h, not swept. Refunds out: after close, total under target, not swept. From the close to close+1h the last two doors are both open.

[Feynman: rescueExcess] Owner, after every campaign’s close+1h. Sweeps only the slack above the owe-number.

[Inversion: rescueExcess] (1) Sweep during grace — locked. (2) Sweep escrowed money — only the slack is movable. (3) Open a campaign with close = max uint64 — lock never lifts.

[Feynman: GoalVault views] `status` treats “total ≥ target” as success even after the close. `isSuccessful` is the same predicate. `isRefundable` / `refundableAmount` need close passed, not swept, and total still under target. `contributionOf` is the backer’s credit (zero after refund, unchanged after a success sweep).

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:375 — why?] Why does withdraw send the historical total rather than “tokens still sitting here for this campaign”? Implicit belief: nothing can leave a campaign except this withdraw. Refunds break that.

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:530 — why?] Why does “failed” mean “historical total ≥ target is impossible,” when refunds never reduce that total? Implicit belief: after the close, the total is frozen. That belief is false — the relayer can still add to it for another hour.

[Socratic: GoalVault.sol:522 — why?] Why is “already swept” enough to refuse a late mint write-down? Implicit belief: a closed campaign has no remaining claimant for that mint. The burner on the other chain still exists.

## Step 1 — Invariant map

### RotatingVault

| ID | Kind | Statement | Writers of each term |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RV-1 | Conservation | For one token: `balance == Σ_circles (Σ_m totalDeposited[c][m] − claimedCount[c]·A[c]·N[c])` (equality ignores donations) | deposit += `totalDeposited`; claim += `claimedCount`, pays `A·N`; refund writes `totalDeposited = claimedCount·A`, pays the delta |
| RV-2 | Coupling | Every claimed pot was funded by all N members, so each member’s consumed share is exactly `claimedCount · A` | claim requires `roundFundedCount[r] ≥ N` then increments `claimedCount`; refund/refundableAmount read that floor |
| RV-3 | All-or-nothing | Round r pays `A·N` to member r, or pays nobody | only `_claimTo`; gated by `fundedCount ≥ N` and not dead |
| RV-4 | Capacity | `fundedCount[r] ≤ N`; one deposit per member per live turn | `_deposit` only; `AlreadyDeposited`; start requires `joined == N` |
| RV-5 | Induction | If any finished turn is short, the latest finished turn is short | deposits target `_elapsedRounds` only and call `_lazyBroken` first |
| RV-6 | Break unwind | On death, unclaimed funded pots dissolve into refunds (no claim) | claim refuses dead; refund ignores `roundDeposits` and uses the floor |
| RV-7 | Permissionless pull | `claimFor` / `refundFor` / `depositFor` cannot redirect value | recipient is `payee` / `member` |
| RV-8 | View/write | `refundableAmount` matches `_refund` math when dead; 0 otherwise | same `held − floor` |
| RV-9 | Status coupling | stored `Active` + last-finished short ⇒ derived Broken | `circleStatus` / mutators apply `_lazyBroken`; `getCircle` does not |

### GoalVault

| ID | Kind | Statement | Writers of each term |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GV-1 | Conservation (advertised) | `token.balanceOf(this) ≥ totalEscrowed` always | `_credit` += both sides; withdraw/refund/refundCrossChain `totalEscrowed -=` then transfer/burn |
| GV-2 | Conservation (implied, **false**) | A campaign’s remaining escrow == `raised` until success-withdraw | `_credit` += `raised` + `totalEscrowed`; **refund decreases only `totalEscrowed`** |

## Step 2 — Break / hold

**RotatingVault RV-1…RV-8 hold** on every fund path. Concrete unit traces (`test_failedFirstRound_everyDepositorRefundedExactly`, `test_failedMidCircle_refundsExcludeAlreadyPaidPots`, `test_brokenCircle_foreclosesUnclaimedEarlierPot`, both fuzzes) drain the vault to zero. `_lazyBroken` induction survives time-skips: later windows sit at fundedCount 0, so last-finished is short. `claimFor` cannot pay the caller (harness `echidna_no_stray_payout`). The missing mirror that GoalVault needs is already here: refund *writes* `totalDeposited = claimedCount · A` and claim pays `A · N`, never a lifetime sum.

RV-9 is a documented reader split, not a fund break (LEAD).

**GoalVault GV-1 can hold while GV-2/GV-3/GV-4/GV-8 die.** `totalEscrowed -= c.raised` on withdraw keeps the *global* `balance ≥ totalEscrowed` number true even after the vault has paid another campaign’s tokens to this beneficiary. The advertised invariant is the wrong conservation law.

**Commutativity break (GV-2/GV-4):** `recordContribution` then `refund` (late mint saves the campaign, refunds close, withdraw works) versus `refund` then `recordContribution` (historical `raised` still includes the refunded pile, grace credit can cross the goal, withdraw overpays or reverts, leftover refunds die). Control the order, extract or freeze.

**Grace vs emergency (GV-6):** `withdraw` is an emergency close that the grace window does not survive.

**Cap bypass (GV-7):** `DeadlineTooFar` is declared and never checked.

**Harness gap:** `GoalVaultFuzz` ghosts treat `raised` as lifetime credits (`ghost_raised` only increments). `echidna_raised_matches_credits` *asserts the bug*. `h_withdraw` swallows a reverting sweep. No property that `status == Succeeded && !withdrawn` implies withdraw can pay `raised` from *this* campaign’s remainder. `RotatingVaultFuzz.echidna_refund_floor` is the view formula compared to itself; RV-1 is the real check.

## RotatingVault — invariants that closed

All-or-nothing per turn (RV-3): claim requires `fundedCount ≥ N`; each deposit is exactly `A`; N members; pot is `A·N`. No partial payout path.

Refund floor (RV-2): every claimed pot was fully funded, so `Σ owed = Σ totalDeposited − N·claimedCount·A` equals the circle’s still-held tokens. Guard `held <= floor` prevents underflow; the unreachable `held < floor` no-ops.

Death induction (RV-5): no deposit path skips `_lazyBroken`. Time-skip past later windows leaves them at 0, last-finished short, unclaimed earlier pots → refunds (documented v1 unwind).

Permissionless pulls (RV-7): `claimFor` / `refundFor` pay the named member only. Harness `ghost_harnessGain` stays 0.

No admin, no rescue clock, no shared `raised`-style lifetime counter on the success path. `MAX_ROUND_DURATION` is the pattern GoalVault’s deadline is missing.
